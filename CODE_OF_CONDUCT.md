# Code of conduct

## Our commitment

We are committed to a harassment-free and welcoming project for everyone, regardless of age,
disability, ethnicity, sex characteristics, gender identity or expression, experience, education,
socioeconomic status, nationality, appearance, race, religion, or sexual identity and orientation.

## Expected behavior

- Be respectful, precise, and constructive.
- Discuss observable behavior and technical trade-offs, not personal characteristics.
- Accept responsibility and correct mistakes.
- Respect privacy and do not publish private information.
- De-escalate disagreements and give others room to participate.

Harassment, threats, sexualized attention, insults, stalking, and sustained disruption are not
acceptable in project spaces or while representing the project.

## Enforcement

Report unacceptable behavior privately to the repository owner through GitHub. Maintainers may edit
or remove content, reject contributions, restrict participation, or permanently ban a participant.
Reports will be handled promptly, fairly, and with attention to reporter privacy and safety.
